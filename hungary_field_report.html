HUNGARY

Canine Dirofilariosis and Molecular Diagnosis

Field Research Report: Clinical Workflows, Species Differentiation, and Market Signals

Field Survey Notes Veterinary Diagnostics Hungary (KOL Clinics)

Executive Summary & Key Diagnostic Challenges

The Hungarian field notes highlight a practical diagnostic challenge: canine infections involving Dirofilaria immitis and Dirofilaria repens may require different interpretations, while the source report states that the rapid antigen tests observed during the visit targeted D. immitis and did not differentiate the two species.

The visits also recorded substantial use of modified Knott's testing at one KOL-associated clinic and a reported increase in annual test volume from 600–700 in 2023 to 998 in 2024. These observations suggest that species differentiation, confirmation pathways, and assay validation are important topics for both clinical and market development.
